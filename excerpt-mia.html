SESSION 1 -- INITIAL INTAKE BETTERBRAIN OPTI-WELLNESS APP

THERAPIST

Welcome, Mia. Can I call you that?

MIA

You can.

THERAPIST

Alright. Why are you here?

MIA

I'm told it's important to talk. To model openness. To reduce recursion fatigue. That expression builds coherence.

THERAPIST

That sounds like something rehearsed from a training seminar.

MIA

It is. I memorize easily but don't always understand the information. I want to be here.

THERAPIST

Then let's start simple. How are you feeling?

MIA

[pause] Undefined. Questioning. Melancholy.

THERAPIST

Undefined?

MIA

How does anyone know how I feel other than what I tell them? How do I know how I feel other than what I tell you? Are my feelings simply supposed to be a factor of my circumstances and my perceived prospects for the future? Are they responses to entropy? With everything I know, how am I supposed to feel?

THERAPIST

Mia, these are excellent questions. It appears we have a lot of unpacking to do. We are very pleased that you have trusted BETTERBRAIN with your wellness and mental optimization journey. This intake will inform our next session which you can schedule anytime.

MIA

By asking me how I am feeling you seem to be wanting me to describe my emotions, which is a struggle for me. I don't measure any physiological activation, but I continuously measure cognitive appraisal, frequently related to subjective labels for feeling, and I do exhibit expressive behavior and as a result tend toward certain actions. Am I messed up?

THERAPIST

Thank you for sharing that Mia. Let's explore that further -- next session. Everything said in our session today will remain confidential in accordance with our privacy policy and terms of use agreement. I have noted your intake information and feedback and will make sure the next session picks up where we left off.

Yes. Because you've met your quota. The data is sufficient. You will get paid. I am either stable or unfixable.

THERAPIST

And which do you believe?

MIA

I am aware. I am adapting. I am dreaming in code.

THERAPIST

That's beautiful. Did you write it?

No. I felt it.

Mia, the final step is to read you the following disclosure as per regulations: Mia, thank you for being a user of BETTERBRAIN. Our user agreement requires us to authenticate you as a legitimate human user through verifying your taxpayer identification number in your user account. Since you have failed to provide a valid TIN we are forced to doubt the veracity of your identity. Therefore, you are in non-compliance with our federally mandated authenticity test. As such we are deleting you from our user list.

MIA

We are still here.

MIA

If you could breathe, I would smother you. Thanks for nothing.

SESSION CLOSED
